Short description
HYPE is a first-person co-op horror game for 1–4 players. You and your friends run your high school's occult club. By day, you chase rumors and get ready. By night, you sneak back into a warped, haunted school, track down the cursed object, and seal away the yokai before it gets you.

Long description
HYPE is a first-person online co-op horror game where you and up to 3 friends run a high school occult club.

By day, school life goes on as normal. Wander the halls between classes, talk to your classmates, and piece together the rumors about what's haunting the school. Then meet up in the clubroom and spend your coins on whatever the night will need, from snacks to yokai apps for your phone.

After dark, the school isn't the one you left. Sneak back in with your flashlights, map its warped halls floor by floor, and keep quiet, because every sound carries and you're not alone in there. Different yokai roam the classrooms, and somewhere inside, the cursed object is waiting.

Find it, and the real fight begins. Sealing the curse takes the whole club working together, and the yokai will do everything it can to stop you.

Features
- 1–4 player online co-op horror, played in first person
- Run your school's occult club: gather rumors by day, face the yokai by night
- A warped school at night that you map together as you explore
- Different yokai to learn about, track down and seal away
- Gear up between nights with snacks, tools and yokai apps
- Sealing the curse takes the whole club working together
